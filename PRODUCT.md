## Platform

## Users

- Owners and buyers at food businesses, sweet shops and bakeries, retailers and small brands in Bangladesh who need branded paper packaging (boxes, bags, stickers, labels) in short to mid runs. _(inferred from the services and portfolio)_
- They usually arrive from Facebook or by word of mouth, often on a phone, and want to see real samples, a minimum order and a fast way to ask for a price over WhatsApp or by phone. _(inferred)_
- Bengali and English readers: the site ships in both languages (/ and /bn/).

## Product Purpose

A marketing site for Prottoya Printing & Packaging, a custom paper printing and packaging maker in Babu Bazar, Dhaka. It shows what they make, proves it with real work, and turns visits into quote requests (WhatsApp, phone or the quote form). Success means more qualified quote requests. _(success metric inferred)_

## Positioning

Paper packaging printed to order from about 500 pieces, with design help and delivery across Bangladesh, plus a physical display centre in Babu Bazar where customers can handle samples before ordering.

## Operating Context

- Customers send size, quantity and logo on WhatsApp or by phone; Prottoya prepares the dieline and artwork, offset-prints (typically on 300 GSM board) and delivers.
- Service area listed on Facebook: Dhaka, Gazipur, Narayanganj, Chittagong, Cox's Bazar, Khulna, Rajshahi, Sylhet, Barisal, Dinajpur.

## Product Principles

1. Show real work first; every claim should be backed by a photo or a fact from the page.
2. One tap to a quote: WhatsApp and phone are always within reach, especially on mobile.
3. Speak both languages equally well.
4. Stay fast on mid-range phones and mobile data.
